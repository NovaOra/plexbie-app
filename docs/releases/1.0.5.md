**New in 1.0.5**
- **One version number:** the app and Plexbie now share version numbers. This app goes with Plexbie 1.0.5; some admin features below need it.
- **Manage menu:** Manage's sections are in a ☰ menu at the top right, each with its count, instead of a picker and a row of links in the middle of the screen.
- **Invite links open in the app.** Someone you invite can accept from the app, without Discord.
- **Links wait for you to sign in.** A Plexbie link tapped while signed out opens once you've signed in. Looking around the sample no longer forgets your saved sign-in or your server's address.
- **Never a blank screen.** Screens say when you're offline or something failed, and offer Try again. Changes you make offline fail straight away instead of hanging. Long imports and scans wait for their answer, and a slow answer is never taken as a no.
- **Drafts are safe.** Leaving a reply or note you haven't sent asks first.
- **Alerts:**
  - tapping one opens its page once, over Home;
  - the You screen checks alerts the same way everywhere, offline too, and can send a test alert;
  - signing out or turning alerts off makes sure your Plexbie hears it.
- **Titles:** opening another title starts with nothing picked. A failed refresh keeps the page you had.
- **Pictures:** a poster that won't load shows a drawn cover. Profile pictures are no longer saved to the phone.
- **Requests:** book requests no longer repeat their type in the heading, and "Updated … ago" on request cards keeps up while the app stays open.
- **Sign-in:** the keyboard no longer pops up over the invite link and the sample on first launch.
- **Live progress (Android):** a late update can't bring back a request that has finished.
- **Easier to use with a screen reader or large text:** messages stay up for 15 seconds, switches read out what they do, search and library counts are announced, and picker buttons fit their text.
- **For admins:**
  - **Sign out every other session**, under Manage → Health. For a sign-in that wasn't you: every other website and app sign-in ends, and alerts turned on elsewhere stop. Needs an up-to-date Plexbie.
  - Cleanup:
    - search Plex for any title to **keep forever**;
    - turning cleanup on offers **Turn on in practice**;
    - a failed save puts back only what it changed.
  - Holds can be confirmed in a dialog instead, for anyone who can't press and hold.
  - Manage:
    - shows services that are down and titles about to leave next to the section picker;
    - runs admin actions side by side;
    - only loads its counts for admins.
  - People asks you to confirm a Plex account match.
  - A new invite link survives switching sections.
  - Blocked imports wait for another show's episodes and start over after a refusal.
  - All requests loads 50 at a time.
  - Back closes a conversation in Messages.
- The sample household starts fresh on each visit, uses the website's free catalog, and tells one consistent story on every screen.

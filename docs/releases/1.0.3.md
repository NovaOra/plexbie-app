**New in 1.0.3**
- **Live progress (Android):** while one of your requests downloads, it stays in your notifications with a bar that fills up until it's on Plex. On Android 16 it's a Live Update, a chip in the status bar. Nothing shows while a request is still waiting, and a stuck download goes by itself. Turn it on or off on the You screen, where **Show me** plays a preview. It needs alerts on, and a Plexbie that sends app alerts.
- **Vibration:** taps, switches and answers each have their own feel, and sending a request gets a little fanfare. On Android the app now uses your phone's own haptics, which are much easier to feel. A Vibration switch on the You screen turns all of it off.
- **Alerts buzz (Android):** alerts have a tap-tap-buzz of their own. Phones with alerts on move over by themselves.
- **For admins:** with an up-to-date Plexbie, every new request and join request reaches your phone the moment it's made, not only Discord.
- The sample household ("Look around with sample data") now shows real, free films, shows and books: Blender Foundation open movies, Pepper & Carrot, and public-domain classics.

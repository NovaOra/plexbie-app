**New in 1.0.3**
- **Live progress (Android):** while one of your requests downloads, it stays in your notifications with a bar that fills up until it's on Plex. On Android 16 it's a Live Update, a chip in the status bar. Nothing shows while a request is still waiting, and a stuck download goes by itself. Turn it on or off on the You screen, where **Show me** plays a preview. **In the status bar** chooses between the % in the status bar (at the top of your notifications) and a quiet notification with your silent ones. It needs alerts on, and a Plexbie that sends app alerts.
- **Vibration:** taps, switches and answers each have their own feel, and sending a request gets a little fanfare. On Android the app now uses your phone's own haptics, which are much easier to feel. A Vibration switch on the You screen turns all of it off.
- **Alerts buzz (Android):** alerts have a tap-tap-buzz of their own. Phones with alerts on move over by themselves.
- **For admins:** with an up-to-date Plexbie, every new request and join request reaches your phone the moment it's made, not only Discord.
- **For admins: downloads that won't import.** When Sonarr or Radarr won't import a finished download by themselves, Plexbie opens a ticket. It shows their reasons word for word and what's in the download (programs, samples and tiny files are pointed out). You can set which episode or film each file is, its quality, language and release group, or skip a file, then hold to import. Manage → Health lists every one waiting.
- The sample household ("Look around with sample data") now shows real, free films, shows and books: Blender Foundation open movies, Pepper & Carrot, and public-domain classics.

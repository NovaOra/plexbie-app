**New in 1.0.7**
- **Something wrong? Open a ticket** on any film or show that's on Plex and isn't one of your requests: say what's wrong (it won't play, the picture or the sound is off, it's the wrong film or episode, episodes are missing, or something else), and the admins get it straight away, with what Plexbie can see of the file and of how you last played it.
- **Need help? on Home:** a ticket for anything that isn't about one title, and all your tickets, open or solved in the last 30 days. Open one to see what the admins said and to add to it, at any time while it's open; an alert about one opens it.
- **Manage → Tickets** says what each ticket is about (Title, General, or the request's number), shows what Plexbie saw when it was opened, and links a title's ticket to its page and to Plex.
- **Leaderboard:** the top three no longer count the server's owner.
- **Fixed:** swiping a request's "Something wrong?" sheet away while it was still sending no longer closes the screen beneath it.
- Tickets about a title and general tickets need Plexbie 1.0.7; with an older Plexbie the app works as before.

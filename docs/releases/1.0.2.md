**New in 1.0.2**
- For admins, Manage has a **Tickets** section. Every "Something wrong?" and every ticket an admin opens is in one place. Open a ticket to take it, add notes only admins see, reply to the person who asked, wait on their answer, and solve it with a last word or reopen it.
- **All requests** now shows every request, including ones waiting for a decision and declined ones. It opens on the last 30 days, and "Every request since No. 0001" shows the rest.
- **Messages** is an inbox for Plexbie's DMs. It shows both sides of each conversation, and you can reply as Plexbie (signed with your name), mark a conversation done, or add a DM to the person's ticket. Conversations with new messages are counted on the Manage tab, and tapping an alert about a DM opens that conversation.
- When you open a ticket from All requests, "Let them know" now opens a box to write your own message to them.
- If you've asked for help, your request shows the conversation. When an admin asks you something, you can answer straight from the app.
- On Android, the keyboard no longer covers what you're typing.

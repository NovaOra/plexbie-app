**New in 1.0.4**
- **For admins: when a message doesn't reach someone.** Replying on a ticket, solving it with a last message, or opening one to tell the member now says **Not delivered** when it reached nobody, instead of "Sent". The reply is tagged "Not delivered" on the ticket, and Plexbie notes why underneath, so you know to tell them another way. With an up-to-date Plexbie, a member whose Discord DMs are closed gets a phone alert or an email instead.
- The sample household's ticket for Elephants Dream shows how it looks.

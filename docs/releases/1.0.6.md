**New in 1.0.6**
- **Your own Plexbie's address only:** plexbie.com is the Plexbie project's website, not a household's server, so the app no longer takes it (or www.plexbie.com) as a server address. The sign-in screen says so before anything is sent, and invite links at that address are refused, pasted or opened in the app.
- **A sign-in saved at plexbie.com is cleared.** If an older version saved one there, the app removes it on launch with its saved data and alert registration, sends nothing to plexbie.com, and asks you to sign in with your own Plexbie's address.
- Goes with Plexbie 1.0.6.

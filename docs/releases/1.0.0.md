**New in 1.0.0: Plexbie is public**
- Plexbie and its app are now open source and work with anyone's Plexbie. Signing in asks for your domain and fills in plexbie.yourdomain.com, or any address you choose.
- App alerts come from your own Plexbie when it sends them; if it doesn't, the app opens your Plexbie's website, whose alerts work on any phone.
- Version numbers start over at 1.0.0 for the public release. It still installs over the version you have.

**New in 1.0.8**
- **A new look:** keys you can press, with a lip that sinks when you tap. Quieter buttons are hollow pills, and each screen has one pink key for the main thing to do. Things that are loading keep their shape, so nothing jumps when they arrive.
- **Sign in on the TV set:** the sign-in screen is a little retro TV. Channel 1 signs you in with Discord or Plex. Channel 2 shows how to get in if you're new.
- **Find:** the search tab is now called Find, so it no longer sits as "Request" beside Requests. Every tab now shows its name.
- **Home:** the films and shows that just arrived give up after a few seconds if Plex is slow, and say so, and the rest of Home doesn't wait for them. Your tickets show the film's or show's poster.
- **Hold to confirm:** tap once and the key says "Tap again to decline" (or whatever it does), then tap again. Holding it still works.
- **For admins: Manage is now a desk.** Five lit monitors show requests, help, people, the library and services at a glance. One queue has everything that needs you, plus what's waiting and what's done. Open an item to deal with it in a sheet. Everything else is in the menu. An alert opens the request, ticket or blocked import it's about.
- **For admins:** a ticket Plexbie opened itself now says "Plexbie, for <member>", not as if the member wrote it.
- **Fixed:** a rare crash when the app opened on some Android phones.
- Posters on your tickets, alerts that open one item, and tickets Plexbie opened itself need Plexbie from 17 October 2026 or later. With an older Plexbie the app works as before.
